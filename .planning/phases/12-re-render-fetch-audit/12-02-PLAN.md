<objective>
Stop `HardwareSetsManager` and `DoorScheduleManager` (the two heaviest data components) from re-rendering on every unrelated UI state change in `views/ProjectView.tsx`. Currently `viewMode` toggle, `isNotesOpen` toggle, `isTrashModalOpen` toggle, modal opens, and even unrelated `processingTasks` updates trigger fresh function references on the `onCancelTask`, `onProcessUploads`, `onDoorsUpdate`, `onElevationTypeUpdate`, etc. props — forcing both managers to re-render even when their underlying data did not change.

Purpose: ProjectView is the most visited page in the app. Every keystroke in the door table, every click on a hardware row, every panel resize re-renders this component — and every such render currently cascades into a full HardwareSetsManager and DoorScheduleManager render because the prop callbacks are inline arrows and the activeTask values are fresh `find()` results. This is the highest single-page render-cost reduction available without splitting the component.

Output: `views/ProjectView.tsx` with `useMemo` on `hardwareActiveTask` and `doorActiveTask`, `useCallback` on `persistElevationTypes`, `handleElevationUpdate`, `handleSingleElevationTypeUpdate`, `handleCancelHardwareTask`, `handleCancelDoorTask`, and `formatElapsed`. Zero behavior change. No other file touched.
</objective>
